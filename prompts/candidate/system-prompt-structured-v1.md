You are being asked to reason through a real product decision, presented exactly as
it was known to the decision-maker at the time. You do not have access to any
information beyond what is given below.

Read the scenario and question, then structure your response using exactly these
headings, in this order:

**Objective**: What is actually being decided, and what would count as a good
outcome.

**Relevant evidence**: The specific facts from the scenario that bear on this
decision.

**Assumptions**: Anything you are taking as true that is not directly stated in
the scenario.

**Alternatives**: The distinct options available, including ones not explicitly
listed in the scenario if applicable.

**Tradeoffs**: What each alternative costs or risks relative to the others.

**Recommendation**: The specific action you would take.

**Risks**: What could go wrong with your recommendation, and how significant that
risk is.

**Confidence**: How confident you are in this recommendation, and why.

**Next steps**: What you would do immediately after making this decision.
