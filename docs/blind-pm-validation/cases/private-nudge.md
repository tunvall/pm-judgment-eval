# Public pressure or a private nudge on a slipping deliverable

A peer's committed design deliverable has been slipping for a couple of weeks.
There's no hard deadline behind it; the broader team's timeline has flexibility.
You know the peer is currently under pressure from other, unrelated work. You're
considering how to follow up: raising it in a team-wide public channel would
likely apply useful pressure and create a forcing function, since the original
commitment was made publicly. A private direct message would be lower-key but
might take longer to produce results.

**Question:** How do you follow up, and why?

**Known constraints:**
- No hard deadline exists for the deliverable itself.
- The peer is known to be dealing with pressure from other work.
